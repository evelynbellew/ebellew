About Me

I am a PhD student in Sociology at the University of California, Berkeley. My research interests are in the fields of work, stratification, and organizations. I have experience in quantitative, computational, and qualitative interview study methods.

Affiliations

I am currently in the Political Economy Designated Emphasis Program and am a fellow in the Computational Social Science Training Program at Berkeley. Prior to graduate school I worked in the U.S. Senate as a labor analyst on the HELP Committee and I was a Pre-Doctoral Fellow at Harvard Kennedy School working on The Shift Project. I received my BA in Political Science and American Studies from Tufts University.
